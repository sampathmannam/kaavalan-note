### Environment limits and cleanup

The emulator initially stalled its System UI before the baseline app was opened, and its retained crash buffer contains Android system-process failures. The five background Google packages used in the earlier QA isolation were temporarily disabled only on this task-owned AVD. The subsequent baseline, upgrade and post-restart app checks passed; the collected crash buffer contains no `com.kaavalan.note` crash entry. This does not establish the root cause of every emulator failure or validate Google-dependent voice/account flows, physical-phone performance, a full Android OS matrix or TalkBack.

Gmail, Wellbeing, Google search, Android System Intelligence and Google Play services were restored to enabled. The disabled-package list exactly matched its pre-test state (only the already-disabled NFC and GMS supervision packages), and font scale remained 1.0. Cleanup logs are `v2.5.0-emulator-restored.log` and the matching before/after disabled-package lists. The task-owned emulator was then stopped; unrelated emulators and the connected physical phone were not changed. The upgraded synthetic QA installation and test journal remain available for later testing.
