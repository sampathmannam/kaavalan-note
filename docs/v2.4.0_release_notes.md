# KaavalanNote v2.4.0 — Your officer workspace

**Release date:** 2026-09-09
**versionCode:** 49
**Upgrade path:** direct update from v2.3.0 (`com.kaavalan.note`); existing local data is preserved

## What changed

- **Three clear places to work.** Today shows what needs attention, Instructions holds your work, and Contacts explains who each person is and how they relate to your instructions. Settings is available from the top bar.
- **Clear responsibility.** Capture an instruction for yourself, one received from a superior, or one assigned to someone else. A contact is optional for an ordinary note; an unfinished thought is not lost because setup is incomplete.
- **One capture workflow everywhere.** The note bar, shared text, widget, quick-settings tile and reminders feed the same workspace. Drafts survive tab changes and activity recreation.
- **An interface for field use.** Improved hierarchy, readable contacts, grouped settings, large-text and landscape layouts, and keyboard-aware capture keep the primary action accessible.
- **Set the reminder while capturing.** Choose **In 1 hour**, **Tomorrow at 9:00**, **Next week**, or an exact date and time before saving a note.
- **Change your mind later.** Open an instruction to reschedule or clear its reminder. Past custom times are rejected without losing the draft.
- **Act from the notification.** Private Android reminders offer **Done** and **Snooze 1 hour**. Tapping the body opens the matching instruction in Today.
- **No duplicate or stale alerts.** Each instruction owns one unique WorkManager schedule. Editing replaces it; marking an instruction done or dropped cancels it; the worker verifies the stored time and status before notifying.
- **Calendar times are accurate.** **Also add to calendar** appears after a reminder is selected and uses that exact time.
- **The workflow survives interruption.** Reminder and calendar choices are kept with the capture draft across activity recreation, while persisted schedules survive process and device restarts.

## Architecture and privacy

Reminder delivery is local-only. The timestamp is stored in the existing encrypted Room database and scheduled with Android WorkManager. Notifications use private lock-screen visibility and a generic public version. The workspace has one shared capture owner, ID-based live instruction details, and a single reactive read model. Existing notes, contacts and backup formats remain compatible; startup never replaces them with sample data. No analytics, telemetry, remote inference, or database migration was added. Android system speech recognition retains its documented device-dependent privacy boundary.
