# KaavalanNote v2.4.0 — Reminders that follow through

**Release date:** 2026-09-08
**versionCode:** 49
**Upgrade path:** direct update from v2.3.0 (`com.kaavalan.note`); existing local data is preserved

## What changed

- **Set the reminder while capturing.** Choose **In 1 hour**, **Tomorrow at 9:00**, **Next week**, or an exact date and time before saving a note.
- **Change your mind later.** Open an instruction to reschedule or clear its reminder. Past custom times are rejected without losing the draft.
- **Act from the notification.** Private Android reminders offer **Done** and **Snooze 1 hour**. Tapping the body opens the matching instruction in Today.
- **No duplicate or stale alerts.** Each instruction owns one unique WorkManager schedule. Editing replaces it; marking an instruction done or dropped cancels it; the worker verifies the stored time and status before notifying.
- **Calendar times are accurate.** **Also add to calendar** appears after a reminder is selected and uses that exact time.
- **The workflow survives interruption.** Reminder and calendar choices are kept with the capture draft across activity recreation, while persisted schedules survive process and device restarts.

## Architecture and privacy

Reminder delivery is local-only. The timestamp is stored in the existing encrypted Room database and scheduled with Android WorkManager. Notifications use private lock-screen visibility and a generic public version. No analytics, telemetry, remote inference, new network call, or database migration was added.
